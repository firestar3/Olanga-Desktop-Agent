Gemini connection

Used for cloud speech recognition, questions, screen tasks and AI features in Notes and News. Local commands can run without a key.

Checking local connection settings…

Testing sends one short request to Gemini using a saved key. Opening Settings does not send a request.

Speech & voice engines

On-device recognition keeps audio on this device and asks you to review the transcript before sending. It can be slower and less accurate. Questions and screen tasks still use Gemini.

Windows uses your built-in system voice. Magpie sends reply text to NVIDIA to create speech.

NVIDIA connection Optional

Only needed for the Magpie voice. It does not handle reasoning or control your apps.

Save & test sends a short sample to NVIDIA. It does not change your voice engine.

Intelligence & desktop tasks

Gemini handles conversation, screen diagnosis, action planning and final responses.

For “fix the bug on my screen”, Olanga diagnoses the issue, asks for approval, edits only the selected window or editor, and captures the result to verify. A spoken yes opens the native edit review; changing scope requires fresh permission.

Your data & privacy

Local when it can be. Connected when you ask.

Olanga has no automatic analytics uploads. Connected features send the information needed to the service you use. Your provider's own terms apply.

Listening & speech
Wake-word recognition runs on this computer. With cloud recognition, your activated request audio goes to Google Gemini. On-device recognition keeps that audio local and asks you to review the text; submitted questions may still use Gemini. Gemini Live streams audio only during a session you start.
Answers & context
Gemini requests can include recent conversation, your checklist, saved location and enabled memories. Cloud screen assistance sends captures you approve. Magpie sends reply text to NVIDIA when you choose that voice.
Saved on this device
API keys use Windows-backed encryption. Notes, memories, tasks and other saved content are local but are not all encrypted by Olanga. Activity retention and performance measurements are optional.
Your controls
Learning of clear everyday facts is on by default and runs locally. Say pause learning or forget what you just learned, or review and delete memories in Workspace. Choose whether to retain activity and diagnostics. Clear individual saved items where you manage them. Removing local data does not remove copies already held by a provider.
