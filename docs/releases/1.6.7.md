# Olanga 1.6.7

This Windows update brings live NBA scores, Spotify controls and timers to the corner of your desktop. It includes the changes developed since the public 1.6.1 release.

## Follow a game

Enable **Settings → User preferences → Live scores** and choose any of the 30 NBA teams. Golden State is selected initially. During a game, the card shows both scores, the quarter and the game clock. It checks ESPN about every 15 seconds and marks delayed updates. You can also include upcoming games and finals. Scores need no AI key.

## See your timers beside the orb

Under **Settings → User preferences → Timer card**, enable **Pin timers beside Olanga**. Click or drag the grip to switch between up to five expanded timers, a compact next-timer view and a tucked handle. With a game and timer active, switch between them in one spot. The timer card disappears when the last timer is dismissed.

## Keep the taskbar clear

The orb and its card move out of the way while the pointer is over the bottom Windows taskbar. They return when you leave without taking focus. The grip sits inside the score card and tucked scores keep updating. The orb’s five-second and one-hour hide options cover the card too.

## Use Spotify controls on Home

Open Olanga and the floating controls give way to the Home cards. Check the current track, pause or resume, and skip backward or forward through Spotify’s Windows media session. The cards appear only on Home. Music observation pauses when you switch tabs or hide the app.

## Conversation, routines and screen edits

- Say **“Forget where I live”** to remove a saved city. If several memories match, Olanga asks which one you meant. A note containing several facts can be reviewed in Workspace.
- A new command can take over from an unanswered question. Timer, task and memory clarifications keep track of the item being discussed.
- Opening the Routines collection preserves an unfinished routine draft.
- Switching screen-edit examples clears the earlier capture and approval. Undo dismisses pending questions and reports cancellation separately from a failed or expired undo.

To update an existing installation, open **Workspace → Apps & updates**, check for updates, download the verified installer and choose **Install & restart**.
