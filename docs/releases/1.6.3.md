# Olanga 1.6.3

Local build for live scores and the in-app music card. Not published to GitHub Releases.

- Scores appear only during your selected team's live game by default. Background checks keep watching for tip-off. Upcoming games and recent results remain available by switching off **Only show during live games**.
- Drag the floating card's top tab down to tuck it away, and up to restore it. Click, Enter, Space and the arrow keys also work. Updates continue while tucked away.
- A settings button on the score card and **Live scores settings** in the tray open the exact preferences section.
- The corner orb and floating score card hide while Olanga is focused. Cards sit on the right inside the app without resizing or rearranging the page, with Spotify's current track and previous, play/pause and next controls below the scores.
- The in-app cards have a hide/show tab so underlying controls remain accessible. Small windows start with these cards tucked away; expanding them leaves the page in place.
- Spotify status is read locally from its Windows media session and stops polling when the app is hidden. Controls target Spotify specifically. Observation uses a separate read-only worker so it cannot block voice-driven playback commands.
- Existing preferences and app data are retained. The privacy notice documents background score checks and local music status.

Live scores remain opt-in. ESPN data can lag the broadcast. This local installer is unsigned, as no publisher certificate is configured. No automatic update or public release is created.
