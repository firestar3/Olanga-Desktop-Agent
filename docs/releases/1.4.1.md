# Olanga 1.4.1

Olanga 1.4.1 restores a consistent look throughout Workspace and adds a manual update flow inside the app.

## Consistent Workspace controls

The Workspace launcher, dialogs, tabs, buttons and fields now use the same typography, colors and controls as the rest of Olanga. Routine reviews, on-device transcript reviews, Memories and Health follow the same theme, with clearer keyboard focus and layouts that fit smaller windows.

## Updates on your terms

Open **Workspace → Apps & updates**, choose **Check for updates**, then **Download update**. Progress and cancellation stay inside Olanga. Once the download passes verification, choose **Install & restart** to update the existing installation and reopen the app. Settings and saved data stay in place.

There are no automatic checks, background update downloads or installations on quit. Olanga checks the installer checksum after download and again before launching it. Checksum verification does not verify a Windows publisher signature; this release's signing status is stated below.

## Fixes

- Streamed answers report a response-limit cutoff instead of claiming completion.
- A response label split across network chunks is no longer spoken aloud.
- Failed speech playback stops the queue and keeps the full answer visible.
- Failed note imports restore the original note without overwriting another active tab.

## Install or update

Download **Olanga-Setup-1.4.1.exe** from this release. Versions through 1.4.0 need this one installer update to gain the new in-app update flow. Quit Olanga from its tray menu before running the installer, then reopen it. Future releases can be downloaded and installed from **Apps & updates**.
