# Olanga 1.6.5

Local refinement of the floating score card. Not published to GitHub Releases.

- The drag grip is integrated into the card's top edge. The separate protruding tab is gone, keeping the expanded card within its compact 190 × 96 pixel outline.
- Drag down to leave a slim strip at the screen edge; drag up or click the grip to restore scores. Keyboard controls remain available, and tucked scores keep updating.
- Removed the score card's five-second and one-hour hide menu and its timer logic.
- In-app score and Spotify cards remain Home-only, with no hide button or changes to the page layout.

This unsigned local installer preserves existing preferences and app data. No public release or automatic download is created.
