# Olanga 1.6.4

Local refinement of live scores and Spotify cards. Not published to GitHub Releases.

- The floating score card is more compact: 190 × 96 pixels, with the same live scores, settings, refresh control and drag-to-tuck tab.
- Its three-dot menu offers **Hide for 5 seconds** and **Hide for 1 hour**. This hides the floating score card independently of the corner orb; scores remain current for Home.
- In-app scores and Spotify controls appear only on Home. They disappear on Settings, News and Notepad, and return when you go Home.
- Home cards have no hide button and do not resize or move the page. Spotify observation pauses while another tab is open.
- The corner orb and floating scores still hide when Olanga is focused and return when you switch away. Scores retain the live-games-only preference.

This unsigned local installer preserves existing preferences and app data. No public release or automatic download is created.
