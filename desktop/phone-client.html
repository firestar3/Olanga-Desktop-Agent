Pair with your computer

Open Work tools → Phone remote in Olanga. Check that the HTTPS certificate fingerprint matches the one shown there before trusting this connection.

The code works once and expires in five minutes. Five incorrect attempts lock pairing until you restart phone control on the computer.

Ask Olanga

Send one app launch, volume change, or timer. Olanga shows the result after the action finishes.

Typing is the default. Dictation uses your phone browser’s speech service, which may send microphone audio to its provider. Review the transcript, then press Send command. Your keyboard’s dictation also works.

Phone control cannot run shell commands, close apps, browse files, or call a model. Stop it from Olanga whenever you’re done. It stops automatically after one hour.
